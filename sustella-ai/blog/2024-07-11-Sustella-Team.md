## Our story

A shared love for technology, climate, and sustainability brought us together. United by a passion to accelerate decision-making for climate and sustainability challenges, we're creating frontier technology to drive impactful change.


**Arijit** is our senior machine learning and artificial intelligence expert, passionate about the world of generative AI. With a robust science background and extensive industry experience, he is the perfect tech lead to develop and deploy our product at scale.


**Nikolay** is our advisor, offering deep-rooted scientific expertise in climate modeling. He guides us through the complexities of climate science, climate models and risk assessments.

With a background in business and data science, **Tanvi** brings a unique blend of strategic thinking and domain expertise to our product roadmap and development. She has significant experience in decarbonization and sustainability data pipelines and has collaborated with Nikolay on climate modeling projects.


**Josipa** has a strong entrepreneurial and commercial mindset, ensuring everything we do delivers business value with a keen focus on design. Having previously founded climate tech startups, she brings a wealth of experience and a drive for success to Sustella AI.
